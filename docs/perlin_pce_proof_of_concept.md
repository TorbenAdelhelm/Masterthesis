# Perlin PCE proof of concept

This milestone tests whether a low-dimensional non-intrusive polynomial chaos
surrogate can approximate a continuous scalar QoI of the deterministic LGCNN
when permeability is generated by the explicit Perlin stochastic-coordinate
map.

The experiment intentionally precedes the log-Gaussian/KL experiment. Its main
purpose is to separate two questions:

1. can a global PCE approximate the nonlinear LGCNN/streamline response at all;
2. does the pretrained LGCNN remain credible when the permeability law is later
   changed from the Perlin training regime to a Gaussian random field.

## Stochastic input

The input coordinates are

$$
\boldsymbol\xi=(\xi_x,\xi_y),\qquad
\xi_x,\xi_y\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}\mathcal U(-1,1),
$$

and are mapped to permeability through

$$
\boldsymbol\xi
\xrightarrow{G_{\mathrm{Perlin}}}
K(\boldsymbol\xi).
$$

`PerlinCoordinatePermeabilityMap` uses the historical 2-D `perlin_v2` spatial
formula and permeability transformation. The exact interpretation of the two
coordinates versus the finite historical training sequence is documented in
`docs/perlin_stochastic_coordinates.md`.

## Deterministic forward model and first QoI

The frozen release25 LGCNN is treated as

$$
T(\boldsymbol\xi)=F_{\mathrm{LGCNN}}(K(\boldsymbol\xi);p_0,i_0).
$$

The first PCE target is the spatial mean temperature anomaly

$$
Q(\boldsymbol\xi)
=
\frac{1}{|\Omega_T|}
\sum_{x\in\Omega_T}
\bigl(T(x,\boldsymbol\xi)-T_{\mathrm{bg}}\bigr).
$$

This QoI is deliberately continuous. Thresholded exceedance probabilities,
plume area, and plume length are deferred because discontinuous or topology-
changing QoIs are less favorable targets for a first global PCE experiment.

## Legendre basis

For independent uniform coordinates, the one-dimensional orthonormal basis is

$$
\psi_n(\xi)=\sqrt{2n+1}\,P_n(\xi),
$$

where `P_n` is the ordinary Legendre polynomial. The tensor-product basis is

$$
\Psi_{\boldsymbol\alpha}(\boldsymbol\xi)
=
\prod_{j=1}^2\psi_{\alpha_j}(\xi_j).
$$

The first implementation uses total-degree truncation

$$
\mathcal A_p
=
\{\boldsymbol\alpha\in\mathbb N_0^2:\ |\boldsymbol\alpha|_1\le p\}.
$$

The number of coefficients is

$$
P=\binom{2+p}{p}.
$$

Thus degrees 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 contain 6, 10, 15, 21, and 28 terms.

## Regression design

Training coordinates use a randomized Latin-hypercube design in `[-1,1]^2`.
Given expensive LGCNN targets

$$
Q^{(n)}=Q(\boldsymbol\xi^{(n)}),
$$

the coefficients are fitted by ordinary least squares

$$
\widehat{\mathbf c}
=
\arg\min_{\mathbf c}
\|A\mathbf c-\mathbf Q\|_2^2,
$$

with

$$
A_{n,\alpha}=\Psi_{\alpha}(\boldsymbol\xi^{(n)}).
$$

The implementation rejects underdetermined and rank-deficient designs. The
basis matrix condition number and training RMSE are retained as diagnostics.

## Validation/reference ensemble

Validation uses a separate iid

$$
\boldsymbol\xi^{(n)}_{\mathrm{val}}
\sim\mathcal U(-1,1)^2
$$

ensemble and performs the expensive LGCNN evaluation at every validation point.
This ensemble therefore acts as the scalar Monte Carlo reference under exactly
the same permeability law used for PCE. The PCE is evaluated on the identical
coordinates.

This isolates PCE error from a change in input distribution.

The current diagnostics are:

- RMSE;
- MAE;
- maximum absolute error;
- relative L2 error;
- predictive coefficient `Q2 = 1 - SSE/SST`;
- validation Monte Carlo mean and variance;
- analytic PCE mean and variance.

Because the basis is orthonormal,

$$
\mathbb E[\widehat Q]=c_{\mathbf 0},
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{Var}[\widehat Q]
=
\sum_{\boldsymbol\alpha\ne\mathbf 0} c_{\boldsymbol\alpha}^2.
$$

## Software architecture

The proof-of-concept pipeline is

```text
training design xi_train (Latin hypercube)
             |
             v
PerlinCoordinatePermeabilityMap
             |
             v
Release25Surrogate
             |
             v
MeanTemperatureAnomaly
             |
             v
PolynomialChaosRegressor

independent xi_val (iid uniform)
             |
             +--> LGCNN QoI = Monte Carlo reference
             `--> PCE QoI   = prediction
```

`CoordinateQoIEvaluator` preserves the required pairing between stochastic
coordinates and scalar QoIs. It bypasses `MonteCarloRunner` deliberately:
`MonteCarloRunner` aggregates fields and does not need to know the latent
coordinates, while PCE regression must retain `(xi,Q)` pairs.

## Current scientific status

The PCE mathematics and workflow can be tested in CI using exact synthetic
polynomials and a lightweight deterministic surrogate. The release25 CLI is
implemented, but a scientifically meaningful PCE result still requires running
it with the external published LGCNN assets.

The code alone therefore establishes software and mathematical readiness, not
that degree 4 (or any other degree) is adequate for the real LGCNN response.
The actual thesis experiment should compare several degrees and training budgets
and report convergence against the independent LGCNN-MC validation ensemble.

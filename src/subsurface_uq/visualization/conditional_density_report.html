What is being compared?

Reference error compares a sample with the hidden reference. Ensemble deviation compares a sample with the empirical eight-draw mean. Spread describes disagreement among possible fields; it is distinct from reconstruction error.

For permeability, an error of +0.301 in log10 means twice the reference permeability; −0.301 means half. Red means higher permeability or warmer temperature; blue means lower permeability or colder temperature. A neutral colour means agreement.
